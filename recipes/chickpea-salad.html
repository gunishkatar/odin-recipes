Chickpea Salad

Description

A fresh, no-cook salad of chickpeas, crunchy cucumber, tomatoes, and red onion in a tangy lemon and olive oil dressing. It's protein-packed and keeps well in the fridge, so it's good for meal prep or lunch.

Ingredients

  • canned chickpeas (1 can, drained)
  • cucumber (1, diced)
  • cherry tomatoes (1 cup, halved)
  • red onion (¼, diced)
  • lemon juice (2 tbsp)
  • olive oil (2 tbsp)
  • salt
  • pepper

Steps

  1. Rinse and drain the chickpeas.
  2. Combine them with the cucumber, tomatoes, and onion in a bowl.
  3. Whisk the lemon juice, olive oil, salt, and pepper together.
  4. Pour the dressing over the salad and toss.
  5. Chill for 10 minutes if you have time, then serve.
