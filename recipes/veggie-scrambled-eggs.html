Veggie Scrambled Eggs

Description

Soft, fluffy scrambled eggs cooked with wilted spinach and juicy cherry tomatoes. It's a light, protein-rich breakfast that's ready in under 10 minutes.

Ingredients

  • eggs (3)
  • spinach (1 handful)
  • cherry tomatoes (4, halved)
  • olive oil (1 tsp)
  • salt
  • pepper

Steps

  1. Whisk the eggs with salt and pepper.
  2. Heat the oil in a non-stick pan over medium heat.
  3. Cook the tomatoes and spinach for 1 to 2 minutes until the spinach wilts.
  4. Pour in the eggs and gently stir until just set.
  5. Serve right away.
