Peanut Butter Banana Oatmeal

Description

A warm, creamy bowl of oats with nutty peanut butter and cinnamon, topped with sweet banana slices. It's filling and works well as an easy breakfast, especially on cold mornings.

Ingredients

  • rolled oats (½ cup)
  • milk (1 cup)
  • banana (1, sliced)
  • peanut butter (1 tbsp)
  • cinnamon (pinch)
  • honey (optional)

Steps

  1. Combine the oats and milk in a small pot.
  2. Simmer over medium heat for 5 minutes, stirring often.
  3. Stir in the peanut butter and cinnamon.
  4. Top with the banana slices and a drizzle of honey.
