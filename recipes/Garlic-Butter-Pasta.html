Garlic Butter Pasta

Description

A simple, comforting pasta with a rich, savory sauce made from melted butter, fragrant garlic, and parmesan. The starchy pasta water helps the sauce cling to the spaghetti. It takes about 15 minutes.

Ingredients

  • Spaghetti (200 g)
  • Butter (3 tbsp)
  • Garlic (4 cloves, minced)
  • Parmesan (¼ cup, grated)
  • Salt
  • Black pepper
  • Parsley

Steps

  1. Boil the spaghetti in salted water until al dente, then save ½ cup of the pasta water and drain.
  2. Melt the butter in a pan over medium heat.
  3. Sauté the garlic for about 1 minute until fragrant.
  4. Toss in the pasta with a splash of pasta water.
  5. Stir in the parmesan, pepper, and parsley, then serve.
